On November 1, 2026 [Dynamick](https://www.dynamick.dev) officially went live. I'm the backend engineer there, and after months of building quietly, here's what we've been working on.

Dynamick designs and builds custom websites, web apps and the systems behind them. No templates, no recycled layouts and no vibe-coded slop. My part is the half you don't see: the APIs, infrastructure, and data models that determine whether an app still holds up a year after launch and under a heavy workload.

If you want the full picture, read the [official launch post](https://www.dynamick.dev/articles/launching-dynamick). And if you're starting something, or fixing an application that outgrew its shape, say [hello](https://www.dynamick.dev/contact).
